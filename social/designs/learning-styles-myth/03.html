In 2008, four psychologists spelled out how to test it.

1Sort learners by style.
2Randomly give each group different teaching methods.
3Same test for everyone. Each style should win with its own match.

Very few studies had even used that design. Several that did flatly contradicted the idea.

Source: Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer & Bjork (2008), Psychological Science in the Public Interest 9(3).
